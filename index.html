Final Decision Summary

Select Option 1 (Legend of the Seas) if:

You want to experience Royal Caribbean's flagship mega-ship hardware, multi-neighborhood layout, 28+ dining spots, and a private island day at Perfect Day at CocoCay, structured via a payment of $338.62/month per couple ($169.31/person per month) over 12 months.

Select Option 2 (Adventure of the Seas) if:

You want maximum financial efficiency—saving $1,763.34 per couple ($881.67/person)—with a payment of $383.35/month per couple ($191.68/person per month) over 6 months, a classic cruise layout, and extra savings to allocate toward drink packages and shore excursions.
